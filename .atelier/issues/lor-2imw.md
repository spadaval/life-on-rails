## Goal

The drawer's first tab is quick dial: what Sumeet is likely to want right now, replacing Recent and Frequent, which duplicated each other. It shows a card for what's next from LoR, eight apps for this hour, the four people he has talked to most recently, and apps installed in the last three days not yet opened. The mock is `docs/missions/launcher-use/mocks/home-quick-dial.html` (right frame).

## Done when

- [x] Every launchable app that isn't hidden can be ranked, whether or not a tab lists it. Apps are ranked by a pure, tested function of per-app open counts: how often and how lately each app was opened (the existing decaying weight), multiplied by its affinity for the current hour band and day kind (weekday or weekend), smoothed so an app with few opens isn't ranked by one of them. Tests use fixtures: an app opened every morning ranks first at 8:00 and not at 20:00; a weekend-only app ranks on Saturday and not on Tuesday; an app opened once ranks below a daily one.
- [x] The counts come from Android's usage events (each app moved to the foreground, LoR's own and the launcher's excepted) as well as launcher taps, read incrementally from a cursor when the drawer opens and kept in the launcher's own file, since Android keeps events only a few days. Work apps, whose events the main profile can't read, count by launcher taps.
- [x] People: the conversation shortcuts `LauncherApps` gives (as search reads them, cached ones included), newest `ShortcutInfo.lastChangedTimestamp` first, at most one per person (by the shortcut's `Person` key, else its label), changed in the last 14 days, the first four shown with avatar, name and app badge; a tap opens the conversation. Without any, the row is absent. The selection is a pure function, tested with shortcuts of the same person in two apps, an old one and fewer than four.
- [x] The card shows `/device/home`'s `one` (kicker and text) through `LorReads`, greyed with its time when LoR can't be read, and absent without one; a tap does what the header's tap does.
- [x] On `T3_Phone_API35` against the test server, with usage generated by opening apps through `agent-device`: screenshots of quick dial at two hours set on the emulator (morning, evening) showing different order, with people and the card present.
- [x] The spec describes quick dial.
- [x] `assembleDebug testDebugUnitTest` passes on boat.

## Scope

`home/` (the quick tab, the counts), its tests, the spec. No server change; the card's mock "Directions" button is left out, since `/device/home` carries no event location.

### Deviations

- **The score's "how often and how lately" is not LaunchStats' weight.** Each app keeps a count per slot (five hour bands, weekday or weekend). Every open adds 1 and every count halves in 14 days. At usage-event volume, LaunchStats' per-launch decay (α 0.03) fades a morning app to nothing by evening. The strategy lets the orchestrator set the score's weights; please confirm this reading of the Done when line's parenthesis. Decision note of 2026-11-09.
- **People order is newest `lastChangedTimestamp` between publishes, and the app's rank within one.** Messages republishes all its chats within milliseconds whenever one changes, which put the newest chat last. Shortcuts an app declares in its manifest are skipped. The person is told apart by the shortcut's label, because `ShortcutInfo.getPersons()` is a system API.
- **Ranked tiles are the drawer's normal tile size,** not the mock's slightly larger icons.

### Surprises

- Setting the clock moves Android's stored usage events (`UsageStatsDatabase: Time changed by +11h50m… files moved`). A wall-time cursor then counted old opens again, so the cursor now carries `elapsedRealtime` and moves with the clock. It's rare on a phone, but it also happens on a manual time set.
- `LorReads.current` is true after a failed read too. The card greys on `current && error == null`, as the lor panel's lists do.
- The boat VM hung mid-run (unreachable about 30 min) and was resumed from its snapshot. Builds before and after agree.
