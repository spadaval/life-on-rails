## Goal

Sumeet judges the mission's launcher on the real Fold after a few days of use.

## Done when

- [ ] A swipe up of a couple of centimetres opens the drawer, and a short flick does; a scroll on home isn't taken as one.
- [ ] Every long-press menu points at what opened it, on the cover and the inner screen.
- [ ] The tabs and the search button are easy to reach and to tell apart; search grows and folds without a jump.
- [ ] Quick dial shows what he wants at least most of the time over three days; the weights are adjusted if not.
- [ ] Home's thumb rows hold what he opens most.
