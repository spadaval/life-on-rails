## Finding 1: Keep the running block's identity when opening Switch
Recommendation: fix now

`prototype/dashboard/src/directions/timeline/screens/Today.tsx:335` writes `hm(b.t_start)` to `block`, while line 162 resolves it by finding the span containing that minute. A newly started/switched timer normally starts partway through a minute. Its formatted minute is before its actual start, so the lookup selects the previous block or no block. The same truncation is used by the running block's main button at line 320.

Reproduced at both 1440 and 412: reload `#/timeline/?shell=0` without `still=1`, switch the initial timer to Public beta, then press Switch on the new running block. Instead of “Switch from Public beta”, the editor shows the closed 14:13 block, “Draft the offline maps spec”, with relabel and delete controls. Picking a label changes that closed block and leaves the running timer alone. This breaks required interaction 9 during normal live-clock use and can silently edit different time. The frozen fixture and catalogue link conceal it because their initial start is exactly 14:13:00.

Resolve interaction targets by block id or a timestamp that preserves seconds, while retaining the human-readable `block=HH:MM` entry path for catalogue links. Evidence: `/tmp/lor-review-8mbs/findings/timer-wrong-block-1440.png` and `timer-wrong-block-412.png`.

## Finding 2: Keep Today's task popovers out of the column's clipping area
Recommendation: fix now

`prototype/dashboard/src/directions/timeline/components/Popover.tsx:40` clamps the left position to at least 8 px but always renders a 380 px editor inside the nearest pop root. Today's `.tl-untimed` is only 360 px at 1440 and 316 px at 1100, and has `overflow: auto` (`styles.css:1372`). The editor therefore cannot fit, regardless of where in that column it is positioned. Placement also limits the bottom against the root's entire scroll height rather than the visible viewport (Popover line 43).

At 1440x950, open “Reply to the App Store review notes” from Today's Waiting on you. After animation, the editor occupies x=1089..1469 and y=524..1050. Its right side, close control and lower contents are clipped; the right edge of the viewport is 1440 and the bottom 950. At 1100 the right overflow grows to 73 px. Tasks' wider list avoids this, so the catalogue's “Tasks with a task open” does not expose the problem. It affects opening a task from Today, which the brief explicitly requires fixing.

Place the editor in an overlay that can extend beyond the column, and fit its position/size to the visible area while preserving the anchor relationship and scrolling. Evidence: `/tmp/lor-review-8mbs/findings/task-popover-1440.png` and `task-popover-1100.png`.

## Finding 3: Preserve the unreachable state when navigating within Timeline
Recommendation: fix now

`prototype/dashboard/src/directions/timeline/App.tsx:32` derives reachability only from `route.params.state`, but its navigation links at line 74 call the shared `href(x.path)` without that parameter. The shared shell preserves only `scene`, `still` and `shell`. Search results and page/detail links use the same pattern.

Open `#/timeline/?state=stale&still=1`, then press Tasks. The URL becomes `#/timeline/tasks?still=1`, the stale banner disappears and the timer's Stop/Switch controls become enabled immediately, without Try now succeeding. The Report says the warning appears on every page and changes remain off until LoR answers; ordinary navigation currently fakes a recovery. Preserve this direction's state through its own navigation/search/detail paths, or keep the simulated connectivity state outside page-local parameters. Do not require changes to the shared shell for this direction-specific mode.

Evidence: `/tmp/lor-review-8mbs/findings/stale-lost-on-tasks.png`, captured immediately after leaving stale Today through Tasks.
