## Finding 1: Keep tasks under habits represented after removing the goal total
Recommendation: fix now

However, `parentChoices` and the editor allow a task to count toward a habit, and Row gives habits only Did it, without a TaskCount (`:211`). Now they disappear from every task count and task link on that card; selecting the habit does not expose them either, because Details suppresses Tasks for habits.

I created goal "Review habit tasks", habit "Review reading habit" under it, and open task "Buy the reading book" under the habit through the test server's CLI. Preserve a count/link for tasks on supported descendants that have no project/goal row count, while keeping the original requirement that tasks are not repeated in a card total. Extend the flow with this supported ancestry case.
