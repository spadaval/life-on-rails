## Scope

The whole LoR dashboard (Today, Tasks, Graph map + outline, Memory: pages / people / timeline, Capture), desktop first, phone adapted. Operate mode. Dark only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A star atlas worn in Elite: Dangerous's menu language, turned down ("Elite: Mostly Harmless"). The day is an arc you travel along; goals are stars. It refuses the stretched single phone column and the grey-card planner.

OWN-WORLD: Space black #0c0d12, panels #111218, dim orange-brown frames #4a2e14 with 6px chamfers on outer panels. Amber-orange #f08a2c for now, timer, tracked time and selection; solid fills only for the active nav item and one primary action per screen. Cyan #6cc6ec for calendar and agent. Saira Condensed caps for titles, nav, section headers and readouts; Saira sentence case elsewhere. Four-point stars, faint engraved grid.

STORY: The owner sees how much of the waking day is left and what is running, deals with what the agent flagged, checks or un-checks tasks, and sees which goals are getting time, without being scolded.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail nav (208px) with the active item as a solid orange bar and system health at its foot; top bar with page title, search and the Start timer button. Today: the day arc across the top with the remaining time as the big readout and the running timer under the zenith, planned/actual lanes beneath, then three framed panels: Needs you, Due soon, Goals this week.

FORM: Star Atlas fused with Elite: Dangerous, toned down; user-pinned after four rounds (round-one roll assigned the Tide Chart; the user steered to Star Atlas and named Elite: Dangerous). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
