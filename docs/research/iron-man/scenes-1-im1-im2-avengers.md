# Tony Stark and his machines: Iron Man (2008), Iron Man 2 (2010), The Avengers (2012)

Raw catalogue of every interaction between Tony and J.A.R.V.I.S., Dum-E / U / Butterfingers, the suits and the house, plus third parties talking to (or past) JARVIS.

**How to read the quotes.**
- **[V]** verbatim: the line matches a subtitle transcript (Springfield / subslikescript) or my own transcription of a clip. Subtitle files have no speaker names, so who says what is inferred from the scene.
- **[~]** near-verbatim: the subtitle text is garbled (Avengers Springfield subs are poor), so I corrected it against a speaker-labelled script and my memory of the film. Wording may be slightly off.
- **[P]** paraphrase or a description of action on screen.
- **DELETED** marks a deleted or extended scene (Blu-ray extras). Everything else is theatrical.

Position is a rough fraction of runtime (e.g. "~25%").

Tags used for "what the AI is doing": INFORM, WARN, ADVISE-AGAINST, OBJECT, COMPLY-DESPITE-OBJECTION, EXECUTE, ANTICIPATE/UNPROMPTED, BANTER, REMEMBER-CONTEXT, LIFE-MGMT, SIM/ANALYSIS, SILENT, REFUSE, OVERRIDDEN, DEFER.

## IRON MAN (2008)

### IM1-1. Wake-up broadcast to an empty bed (~10%)
- **Scene:** Malibu mansion, morning after Tony's one-night stand with Christine Everhart. Tony is already down in the workshop.
- **Initiator / modality:** JARVIS, unprompted. House systems: the wall glass unfrosts and the voice comes over the room speakers.
- **Lines [V]:** JARVIS: "Good morning. It's 7 a.m. The weather in Malibu is 72 degrees with scattered clouds. The surf conditions are fair with waist-to-shoulder highlines. High tide will be at 10:52 a.m."
- **AI is doing:** LIFE-MGMT, UNPROMPTED. A scheduled morning brief (time, weather, surf, tide) built for Tony's habits, delivered to whoever is in the bed.
- **Relationship:** The routine runs whether Tony is there or not. JARVIS's first appearance is ambient infrastructure, not a character.

### IM1-2. JARVIS stops the guest; "he runs the house" (~10%)
- **Scene:** Christine wanders the house looking for Tony.
- **Initiator / modality:** Triggered by a third party. House voice or panel.
- **Lines [V, speaker partly inferred]:** "You are not authorized to access this area." Christine: "Jesus!" Pepper: "That's Jarvis, he runs the house." [Uncertain whether the refusal is JARVIS's voice or a screen/lock message. The subtitle places it right before Pepper's line.]
- **AI is doing:** REFUSE, i.e. access control for non-owners. A third party meets JARVIS.
- **Relationship:** To guests, JARVIS is security and staff. Pepper introduces him the way you introduce a household employee. Pepper and JARVIS split the "staff" role: she has the dry-cleaning and the car waiting.

### IM1-3. Hot rod in the workshop (~11%)
- **Scene:** Tony under his 1932 Ford hot rod, music blasting. Pepper comes down.
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony. Voice plus holographic display in the workshop.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony: "Give me the exploded view." JARVIS: "The compression in cylinder three appears to be low." Tony: "Note that." Then Tony to Pepper: "Please don't turn down my music."
- **AI is doing:** EXECUTE (displays the model). SIM/ANALYSIS: volunteers a diagnostic Tony didn't ask for. REMEMBER (Tony tells him to note it).
- **Relationship:** A shop assistant and a lab notebook. Tony delegates memory ("Note that") without a second thought.

### IM1-4. "JARVIS, you up?" Opening the Mark II project (~38%)
- **Scene:** After Rhodey turns down Tony's pitch, Tony goes down to the workshop alone.
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony. Voice in the workshop.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony: "Jarvis, you up?" JARVIS: "For you, sir, always." Tony: "I'd like to open a new project file, index as: Mark Two." JARVIS: "Shall I store this on the Stark Industries central database?" Tony: "I don't know who to trust right now. Till further notice, why don't we just keep everything on my private server." JARVIS: "Working on a secret project, are we, sir?" Tony: "I don't want this winding up in the wrong hands. Maybe in mine it could actually do some good."
- **AI is doing:** EXECUTE. ASKS A CLARIFYING QUESTION about data placement: its default is the company system, and it checks. BANTER (gentle teasing).
- **Relationship:** The moment of trust. Tony trusts no humans ("I don't know who to trust"), so the private AI becomes the only collaborator, on a private server. Loyalty is part of the persona ("always"). JARVIS's question is what makes Tony say his reason out loud, which gives JARVIS a confessor role.

### IM1-10. Uploading JARVIS into the suit; first flight (~43%)
- **Scene:** Tony in the full silver Mark II, in the workshop.
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony. The suit HUD comes alive with JARVIS's voice.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony: "Jarvis, are you there?" JARVIS: "At your service, sir." Tony: "Engage heads-up display." JARVIS: "Check." Tony: "Import all preferences from home interface." JARVIS: "Will do, sir." Tony: "Alright, what do you say?" JARVIS: "I have indeed been uploaded, sir. We're online and ready." Tony: "Start the virtual walkaround." JARVIS: "Importing preferences and calibrating virtual environment." Tony: "Do a check on control surfaces." JARVIS: "As you wish." [Flaps flex.] JARVIS: "Test complete. Preparing to power down and begin diagnostics." Tony: "Ah, yes... tell you what. Do a weather and ATC check. Start listening in on ground control." JARVIS: "Sir, there are still terabytes of calculations needed before an actual flight." Tony: "Jarvis... Sometimes you gotta run before you can walk. Ready? And 3, 2, 1." [Flies out of the garage.]
- **AI is doing:** EXECUTE. ANTICIPATE: JARVIS assumes the plan is the cautious one ("Preparing to power down..."). OBJECT / ADVISE-AGAINST ("terabytes of calculations"). Then COMPLY-DESPITE-OBJECTION.
- **Relationship:** The template for the whole series. JARVIS states the risk once, politely and with data. Tony overrules with a slogan, and JARVIS goes along without a second protest. Who decides is never in doubt. "Import all preferences from home interface" makes JARVIS one assistant that moves from house to suit with its context intact.

### IM1-11. The altitude record and the icing (~44%)
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony asks a question; JARVIS answers and later warns unprompted. HUD in flight.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony: "What's the SR-71's record?" JARVIS: "The altitude record for fixed-wing flight is 85,000 feet, sir." Tony: "Records are made to be broken! C'mon!" JARVIS: "Sir, there is a potentially fatal build-up of ice occurring." Tony: "Keep going! Higher!" [The suit freezes and power fails.] Tony: "We're iced up, Jarvis! Deploy flaps. Jarvis? C'mon, we gotta break the ice!" [He free-falls and the ice breaks; power comes back.] Tony: "Cut power." [He crashes through the house roof onto the Shelby Cobra; Dum-E sprays him with the extinguisher. P]
- **AI is doing:** INFORM (when asked). WARN (unprompted, says "fatal"). OVERRIDDEN. Then SILENT/offline: the system freezes, and the "Jarvis?" goes unanswered.
- **Relationship:** Tony uses JARVIS's facts as a dare. The warning is ignored and is then proved right by physics. The first time the AI goes silent is the moment of greatest danger. Dum-E's reflexive extinguisher spray is the callback joke to the "donate you" threat.

### IM1-12. Post-flight debrief and the hot-rod-red render (~46%)
- **Scene:** Back in the workshop, at the holographic design station.
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony dictates; JARVIS renders holograms and answers with dry wit.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony: "Note: main transducer feels sluggish at plus 40 altitude. Hull pressurization is problematic. I'm thinking icing is a probable factor." JARVIS: "A very astute observation, sir. Perhaps if you intend to visit other planets, we should improve the exo-systems." Tony: "Connect to the Sheva [subs: 'Cisco'; uncertain], have it reconfigure the shell metals. Use the gold-titanium alloy from the Seraphim tactical satellite. That should ensure fuselage integrity while maintaining power-to-weight ratio. Got it?" JARVIS: "Yes. Shall I render using proposed specifications?" Tony: "Thrill me."
- **AI is doing:** REMEMBER (a dictated engineering log). BANTER (sarcastic "astute observation", which is an "I told you so"). EXECUTE (connect to the fabricator). Offers the next step ("Shall I render").
- **Relationship:** JARVIS gets a polite "told you so", the only consequence of being overruled. There's no sulking and no "you should have listened". The design authority is Tony's: he picks the material and JARVIS implements.

### IM1-18. Paralysed; Dum-E hands him the reactor (~83%)
- **Scene:** Stane has stolen the arc reactor. Tony drags himself down to the workshop and can't reach the old Mark I reactor that Pepper had framed ("Proof that Tony Stark has a heart"). Dum-E picks it up and holds it out to him.
- **Initiator / modality:** Dum-E, unprompted. Physical.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony (weakly): "Good boy."
- **AI is doing:** ANTICIPATE / UNPROMPTED rescue by the dumbest machine in the house. JARVIS is absent or silent through this scene: no alarm, no call for help [P; nothing in the transcript].
- **Relationship:** The robot Tony insults most saves his life by understanding a need without an instruction. Rhodey arrives only afterwards (Pepper sent him). Contrast this with IM1-4: the private, offline-from-the-world setup means nobody is alerted when Tony is attacked at home. The house does not call anyone.

### IM1-19. Final fight with the Iron Monger: running out of power (~88-92%)
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony asks; JARVIS reports and warns. HUD.
- **Lines [V unless marked]:** Tony: "How do you think the Mark One chest piece is gonna hold up?" JARVIS: "That chest piece was never designed for sustained flight." Tony: "Keep me posted." ... Tony: "Defer [~ 'reroute'] the power to the chest piece." JARVIS: "Power reduced to 19%." JARVIS: "Sir, it appears that his suit can fly." Tony: "Duly noted. Take me to maximum altitude." JARVIS: "With only 15% power, the odds of reaching that altitude..." Tony: "I know the math! Do it!" JARVIS: "13% power, sir." Tony: "Climb!" JARVIS: "11%." Tony: "Keep going!" JARVIS: "7% power." - **Relationship:** Under stress, Tony controls how JARVIS talks to him (cut the spoken countdown, keep the visual). The odds are known and acknowledged ("I know the math"), and the decision stays human. The winning move (icing) comes from the failure JARVIS warned about in IM1-11, so the earlier experience pays off.

## IRON MAN 2 (2010)

### IM2-1. Senate hearing: Tony hijacks the screens (~8%)
- **Modality:** Tony uses his transparent phone to take over the committee's displays and play footage of foreign suit attempts [P]. JARVIS is not voiced. The phone and its backend do the hacking.
- **Relationship:** The assistant's power used as theatre. Delegated hacking for a public argument.

### IM2-2. "Daddy's home": welcome home, palladium status, and the "Mute" (~12%)
- **Scene:** Tony gets back to the workshop after the Senate hearing. He is quietly dying of palladium poisoning.
- **Initiator / modality:** Tony greets the house; JARVIS replies and then briefs him unprompted. Workshop voice plus a holographic medical readout.
- **Lines [V]:** Tony: "Wake up. Daddy's home." JARVIS: "Welcome home, sir. Congratulations on the opening ceremonies. They were such a success, as was your Senate hearing. And may I say how refreshing it is to finally see you in a video with your clothing on, sir." Tony [to Dum-E, who is fussing or serving the chlorophyll drink, P]: "You! I swear to God I'll dismantle you. I'll soak your motherboard. I'll turn you into a wine rack." Tony: "How many ounces a day of this gobbledegook am I supposed to drink?" JARVIS: "We are up to 80 ounces a day to counteract the symptoms, sir." Tony: "Check palladium levels." JARVIS: "Blood toxicity, 24%. It appears that the continued use of the Iron Man suit is accelerating your condition." [Tony pulls out a burnt palladium core.] JARVIS: "Another core has been depleted." Tony: "God, they're running out quick." JARVIS: "I have run simulations on every known element, and none can serve as a viable replacement for the palladium core. You are running out of both time and options. Unfortunately, the device that's keeping you alive is also killing you." JARVIS: "Miss Potts is approaching. I recommend that you inform her..." Tony: "Mute."
- **AI is doing:** UNPROMPTED greeting with a news recap. BANTER (the clothing jab). LIFE-MGMT / HEALTH: dosing ("80 ounces a day"), monitoring blood toxicity, linking his behaviour (the suit) to the decline. SIM/ANALYSIS: tried every known element; the conclusion is blunt. ANTICIPATE (announces Pepper before she arrives). ADVISES on a personal relationship (tell Pepper). Then OVERRIDDEN / SILENCED with "Mute".
- **Relationship:** The most important scene for the study. JARVIS knows Tony is dying and so far is the only one who does. He says it with no softening ("You are running out of both time and options"). He then makes the one personal recommendation in the trilogy, that Tony tell Pepper, and Tony literally mutes him. The AI can push but not disclose. Tony's secret stays a secret because JARVIS stays silent when told, even with Pepper standing right there. (In the scene that follows, JARVIS says nothing while Pepper and Tony argue.)

## PATTERNS ACROSS THE THREE FILMS

1. **Object once, then comply. Tony always decides.** JARVIS states a risk a single time, with data, and never escalates: "terabytes of calculations" then the flight (IM1-10); "potentially fatal build-up of ice" (IM1-11); "We are unclear as to the effects..." cut off by "I don't want to hear it" (IM2-12); "Mark VII is not ready for deployment" then "skip the spinning rims" (AV-8); "we will lose power before we penetrate that shell" then Jonah (AV-11). JARVIS never refuses Tony in these three films. He only refuses outsiders (IM1-2, and the suit security in the IM2 "Mark II Security" deleted scene).
2. **Pushback turns into wit.** The disagreement comes as dry sarcasm, not argument: "A very astute observation, sir" (the told-you-so after the icing, IM1-12); "that should help you keep a low profile" (IM1-14); "Am I to include the Belgian waffle stands?" (IM2-9); "I wouldn't consider him a role model" (AV-11). The joke lets the AI record its dissent without slowing Tony down, and Tony enjoys it.
3. **Tony controls the channel, not just the content.** He shapes how the AI talks to him: "Mute" (IM2-2), "Just leave it on the screen, stop telling me" (IM1-19, moving from voice to the HUD under stress), "Close the line" (AV-2), "Delete all" (IM1-5 deleted). A good assistant here is one that can be told to be quiet and stays quiet.
4. **The AI notices what matters to Tony as a person, but cannot act on it alone.** JARVIS tracks his health (80 oz a day, 24% toxicity, "running out of both time and options", IM2-2; vitals, IM2-15), recommends he tell Pepper and is muted (IM2-2), and offers the call to Pepper at the end (AV-12). He never tells Pepper himself. The secret leaks through Tony's own open mic (IM2-15). Private data stays private even when disclosing would be kind.
5. **One assistant, carried everywhere, with context.** "Import all preferences from home interface... I have indeed been uploaded" (IM1-10): the same JARVIS runs the house (IM1-1), the workshop, the suit HUD, the phone (AV-2, IM2-12), and covert jobs on someone else's network (AV-6). Continuity of context is the point: "Where did our saga last leave us?" / "485 simulations" (IM2-3 deleted); "Plan B" and "anytime now" need no explanation (AV-8, AV-9).
6. **Trust grows from instruction to intent.** IM1 has step-by-step commands ("Engage heads-up display", "Do a check on control surfaces"). By The Avengers Tony gives a goal or a prod and trusts JARVIS with timing and method: "Jarvis, anytime now" before being thrown out a window (AV-9); "put everything we got into the thrusters" / "I just did" (AV-12, JARVIS already ahead of him).
7. **Background jobs that report when done.** "Query complete, sir" on Vanko (IM2-6); "Jarvis has been running it since I hit the bridge" (AV-6); "Commencing automated assembly... five hours" / "Don't wait up for me, honey" (IM1-14); "I will begin running diagnostics" unprompted (IM2-11). JARVIS gives honest partial results: "Call trace incomplete" (IM2-12), "Remote reboot unsuccessful" (IM2-13).
8. **Ambient, unprompted, domestic.** JARVIS volunteers things nobody asked for: the morning weather and surf (IM1-1, delivered to a guest), "Welcome home" with a news recap and a jab (IM2-2; IM1-5 deleted), "Miss Potts is approaching" (IM2-2), the low cylinder compression (IM1-3), the 400% power (AV-5). He also runs the social side: invitations (IM1-13), call screening (AV-2), inbox triage (IM1-5 deleted).
9. **The secret-keeping and private-server design has costs.** "Keep everything on my private server" (IM1-4) buys secrecy. But when Stane paralyses Tony at home, the house raises no alarm and calls nobody; a dumb robot arm saves him (IM1-18). When S.H.I.E.L.D. wants in, JARVIS is overridden anyway ("my protocols are being overridden", AV-2). And at the drunk party, nothing stops Tony misusing the suit (IM2-7). The AI's deference cuts both ways.
10. **Dum-E is the emotional counterweight.** The physical, dim helper gets constant abuse ("You are a tragedy", "donating you to a city college", "I'll turn you into a wine rack") and rescues Tony unprompted in the one moment JARVIS can't (IM1-18, "Good boy"). Its overeager intervention (spraying him when he isn't on fire) is the comic flip side of JARVIS's restraint. Tony has to tune when it should act ("If something happens, then come in", IM1-8).
11. **Silence at the edges.** JARVIS goes quiet exactly when things are worst: the iced-up free fall (IM1-11, "Jarvis?" unanswered), out of power in the Stane fight (IM1-19), and the dead suit beyond the portal (AV-12/13). Each film's peak danger is the moment the assistant is gone, and a human then makes the critical move (Pepper's bypass button, IM1-20; Natasha's reboot, IM2-14; Hulk catching Tony).
12. **JARVIS is talked about to third parties as a person-shaped agent.** Pepper: "That's Jarvis, he runs the house" (IM1-2). Tony to Steve: "Jarvis has been running it since I hit the bridge" (AV-6). Natasha cites "Mr Stark's database security guidelines" (IM2-7). Other people meet JARVIS mostly as a gatekeeper (Christine, Coulson), never as someone they can give orders to.
