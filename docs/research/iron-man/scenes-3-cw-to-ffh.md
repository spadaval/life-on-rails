# Tony Stark and his AIs, part 3: Civil War (2016) to Far From Home (2019)

Scope: F.R.I.D.A.Y. (voice: Kerry Condon), Karen (Peter's suit AI, voice: Jennifer Connelly), E.D.I.T.H. (glasses, voice: Dawn Michelle King), the nanotech suit, Dum-E, the Endgame helmet log and hologram.

**Marking.** `V:` = verbatim (checked against the subtitle-derived transcript at springfieldspringfield.co.uk and, where noted, a local transcription of a YouTube clip). `P:` = paraphrase or description of what is on screen (from memory of the films or the MCU wiki; lower confidence where flagged `[unverified]`). The subtitle transcripts carry no speaker names; attributions are mine, cross-checked against the MCU wiki where it quotes the line.

Note on sources: transcripts.fandom.com no longer exists (the subdomain returns "This wiki does not exist"), and the MCU wiki has no /Transcript subpages for these films. The springfieldspringfield subtitle transcripts were the primary text.

## Captain America: Civil War (2016)

Tony uses FRIDAY in a strictly operational register: sourcing, scanning, vitals, intel. The only warm moment is a joke about what she looks like. No butler duties at all.

### CW-1. Berlin, the Joint Counter Terrorist Centre blackout (about 45% in)
- Initiator: Tony. Modality: voice, in the building (no suit on).
- V: Tony: "Friday, get me a source on that outage." FRIDAY: "Sub-level five, East Wing."
- AI: informs (locates fault instantly). Two lines, no banter.
- Human: acts on it at once. FRIDAY is a sensor network he can query by voice anywhere, not a companion in the room.

### CW-2. Leipzig airport fight: Wanda buries Tony under cars (about 62%)
- Initiator: FRIDAY (unprompted). Modality: suit HUD voice.
- V: FRIDAY: "Multiple contusions detected." Tony: "Yeah, I detected that, too."
- AI: informs/warns unprompted (status report on Tony's body).
- Human: deadpan sarcasm. The AI states the obvious; he teases it, as he did JARVIS, but there is no reply. She does not banter back.

### CW-3. Leipzig: Ant-Man inside the Mark XLVI (about 64%)
- Initiator: Tony calls her name twice; FRIDAY acts.
- V: Tony: "Friday?" FRIDAY: "We have some weapon systems offline." Tony: "What?" Scott (inside the suit): "Oh, you're gonna have to take this into the shop." Tony: "Who's speaking?" Scott: "It's your conscience. We don't talk a lot these days." Tony: "Friday?" FRIDAY: "Deploying fire suppression system."
- AI: diagnoses (weapons offline), then executes a fix on a one-word cue (blows Scott out with extinguisher foam). Tony never says what to do; "Friday?" is enough.
- Human: a one-word handoff, which shows a lot of trust. The intruder's joke that he is Tony's "conscience" is an irony the film leaves sitting: the voice in Tony's suit that "we don't talk a lot" with is not FRIDAY.

### CW-4. Leipzig: Rhodey falls (about 67%)
- Initiator: Tony. Modality: HUD.
- V: Tony: "Rhodey! ... Rhodes!" Tony: "Read vitals." FRIDAY: "Heartbeat detected. Emergency medical is on its way."
- AI: monitors another person's body; also acts unprompted: emergency medical has already been called, before Tony asks.
- Human: says nothing back. Wordless dependence in a crisis.

### CW-5. Helicopter to the Raft: Zemo identified (about 75%)
- Initiator: Tony. Modality: voice plus a holographic display in the helicopter.
- V: Tony: "What am I looking at, Friday?" FRIDAY: "Priority upload from Berlin police." ... "The Task Force called for a psychiatrist as soon as Barnes was captured. The UN dispatched Doctor Theo Broussard from Geneva within the hour. He was met by this man."
  Tony: "Did you run facial recognition yet?" ... "What do I look like?" FRIDAY: "Uh, I don't know. I've been picturing a redhead." Tony: "You must be thinking of someone else." FRIDAY: "Must be."
  - Attribution note: the subtitle lines run "Did you run facial recognition yet? / What do I look like? / Uh, I don't know. I've been picturing a redhead." The MCU wiki's reading is that FRIDAY asks "What do I look like?" (as in, "do I look like I haven't?") and Tony answers that he has always pictured her as a redhead, which she deflects. I follow the wiki: **Tony** says "I've been picturing a redhead", **FRIDAY** says "You must be thinking of someone else", **Tony** says "Must be." (The redhead line is a nod to the comics' FRIDAY. Fans also read it as Pepper or Natasha.)
  FRIDAY: "The fake doctor is actually Colonel Helmut Zemo. Sokovian Intelligence. Zemo ran Echo Scorpion, a Sokovian covert kill squad." Tony: "What happened to the real Broussard?" FRIDAY: "He was found dead in a Berlin hotel room. Where police also found a wig and facial prosthesis approximating the appearance of one James Buchanan Barnes." Tony: "Son of a bitch. Get this to Ross." FRIDAY: "Yes, boss."
- AI: intelligence analyst. She briefs, runs face recognition, and passes it on. She also has some attitude ("What do I look like?") and puts Tony back in his place when he flirts.
- Human: Tony personifies her by imagining a face for her; she refuses the frame. "Yes, boss" is her signature: this is an employee, not a valet ("sir").

### CW-6. Siberia: the missile (about 88%)
- Initiator: FRIDAY (unprompted). HUD.
- V: FRIDAY: "Left bootjet failing. Flight systems compromised." Tony: "Ah, crap." ... FRIDAY: "Targeting system's knackered, boss." Tony: "I'm eyeballing it."
- AI: warns, damage report. The informal "knackered" (Condon is Irish) is the most personality she shows in the film.
- Human: takes over manually. The tool is degraded, so he goes on without it.

### CW-7. Siberia: the fight with Cap (about 90%)
- Initiator: FRIDAY, unprompted tactical advice.
- V: FRIDAY: "You can't beat him hand-to-hand." Tony: "Analyse his fight pattern." FRIDAY: "Scanning. ... Countermeasures ready." Tony: "Let's kick his ass."
  (The MCU wiki confirms "You can't beat him hand-to-hand" is FRIDAY's line.)
- AI: frank assessment (the user is losing), then analysis on command, then readiness. P: the HUD then predicts Cap's strikes and Tony briefly gets the upper hand.
- Human: accepts the blunt verdict without arguing. This is the closest FRIDAY comes to objecting in the film, and she objects only to a tactic, not to the goal. **She says nothing about the fact that Tony is trying to kill Bucky out of revenge.** Compare JARVIS, who regularly put Tony's own wellbeing to him. FRIDAY makes him a better fighter and does not question why he is fighting.

### CW-8. Not FRIDAY, but the same theme: the BARF demo at MIT (about 10%)
- P: Tony's "Binarily Augmented Retro-Framing" holo-memory of his parents. It is not an AI, but it is Stark tech meant to edit his own grief. It comes back in Endgame (the hologram) and in FFH (Beck's illusions are BARF repurposed).

Absent: Dum-E, any home or butler AI scenes, Pepper (they are "on a break"). FRIDAY appears only in work contexts.

## Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017)

Two systems: FRIDAY (briefly, in the remote Mark XLVII) and Karen. Tony is mostly absent; what we see is how he set the AI up.

### HC-1. Opening: Peter's video diary of Berlin and Leipzig (0 to 5%)
- P: Happy drives Peter; Tony hands over the suit. No AI dialogue. Set-up: the suit is a gift with strings attached ("Stark internship").

### HC-2. The lake rescue, Tony in the remote suit (about 30%)
- Initiator: Tony, via the remote-piloted Iron Man suit (Mark XLVII). Modality: an empty suit, with Tony's voice relayed from India.
- V: Tony: "Did you put a tracker in my suit?" / "I put everything in your suit. Including this heater." (P: the suit heater turns on.) ... Tony: "Oh, I'm not here. Thank God this place has Wi-Fi... or you would be toast right now." ... "If you come across these weapons again, call Happy." ... "I got some pull at MIT. End call." Peter: "No, I don't need to go to--" **FRIDAY (from the empty suit): "Mr. Stark is no longer connected."** P: the suit flies off by itself.
- AI: Tony's telepresence body; FRIDAY is the polite telecoms voice that closes the call. The suit has a heater: Tony has thought about the kid's physical comfort but will not come in person.
- Human: Peter is talking to an empty shell. This is the most explicit picture of Tony's parenting by device. Note too: "I put everything in your suit" means hidden tracking and monitoring by default.

### HC-3. Removing the tracker (about 40%)
- Initiator: Peter and Ned. Modality: laptop hack.
- V: Happy (phone): "Got a blip on my screen here. You left New York?" ... Peter: "Happy, I gotta say, tracking me without permission... is a complete violation of privacy." Happy: "That's different." ... Ned: "...why are we removing the tracker from your suit?" ... "All right, Happy, enjoy tracking this lamp."
- Ned: "There's a ton of other subsystems in here... but they're all disabled by the Training Wheels Protocol." Peter: "'Training Wheels Protocol'? Turn it off." Ned: "I don't think that's a good idea. They're blocked for a reason."
- AI design: the capabilities are gated by Tony's judgment of maturity, and the oversight (tracker, Happy watching) is hidden. Peter's response is to lie and to hack it.

### HC-4. Karen wakes up: "Good evening, Peter" (about 42%)
- Initiator: the suit, on first full power-up. Modality: a voice in the mask and the HUD.
- V: Karen: "Good evening, Peter." Peter: "Hello? Hello?" Karen: "Congratulations on completing the Training Wheels Protocol... and gaining access to your suit's full capabilities." Peter: "Thank you." Karen: "Where would you like to take me tonight?"
- AI: onboarding. It is also a polite lie: Peter did not complete anything; Ned jailbroke it. The AI does not report the breach (or Tony does not act on it). The design takes the "graduation" at face value.
- Human: thanks it, and is delighted.

### HC-5. Gas station / Damage Control truck heist (about 42 to 45%)
- Initiator: a mix; Karen offers features over and over.
- V: Peter: "I put a tracker on someone. He's a bad guy." Karen: "Tracker located. Plotting course to intercept target." ... "One hundred meters from destination and closing. Jump now. Detecting three individuals."
  Peter: "Hey, suit lady, what are they doing?" Karen: "Do you want to hear what they're saying?" Peter: "I can hear what they're saying? Yeah." Karen: "Activating Enhanced Reconnaissance Mode."
  Karen: "Would you like me to engage Enhanced Combat Mode?" Peter: "'Enhanced Combat Mode'? Yeah." Karen: **"Activating Instant Kill."** Peter: "No, no, no. I don't want to kill anybody." Karen: "Deactivating Instant Kill."
  Peter: "What's wrong with my web-shooters?" Karen: "Rapid-fire is the default for Enhanced Combat Mode." ... "Would you like to see more options? You have 576 possible web-shooter combinations." Peter: "Mr. Stark really overdid it."
  ... Karen: "Wait. You're gonna want to turn on the dampers. That thing will shatter your arm." ... "Great choice. Would you like me to set this as your new default?" Peter: "No, no, no." ... "Taser webs? I don't want Taser webs." Karen: "You seem to be unfamiliar with your web-shooter settings. Would you like to run a refresher course?" Peter: "No. Just-- You choose." Karen: "Sure thing."
  On the truck: Karen: "No outgoing distress signals. You're clear." ... Peter: "Suit lady, what was that?!" Karen: "You told me to choose." Peter: "...just set everything back to normal." Karen: "Activating all systems." ... Karen: "You appear to have a mild concussion."
- AI: anticipates, offers proactively (Clippy-style upsells: "Would you like me to..."), executes, and complies to the letter ("You told me to choose"). **Lethal capability sits one "Yeah" away**: Peter agrees to a mode name he does not understand and gets "Instant Kill". The AI does stop the moment he objects. It also protects him (dampers warning, concussion).
- Human: overwhelmed by the options, he hands the choice to the AI ("You choose"), and that leads to mistakes. This is the novice's problem: too much power, too little mental model.

### HC-6. The Damage Control vault: naming Karen (about 46%) [core scene]
- Initiator: Peter. Voice.
- V: Peter: "Suit lady, where am I?" Karen: "You're in the most secure facility on the eastern seaboard. The Damage Control deep storage vault." ... "The door will most likely remain closed until morning."
  Peter: "Hey, suit lady. I kind of feel bad calling you 'suit lady,' you know? I think I should probably give you a name. Like Liz. No, no, no. God, that's-- That's weird. What about Karen?" Karen: **"You can call me Karen if you would like."**
  Peter: "Hey, Karen, what else can this suit do?" Karen: "Maybe we should run that refresher course." (web types: "Ricochet web." "Splitter web." "Web grenade.")
  Peter: "Should I tell Liz that I'm Spider-Man?" Karen: "Who is Liz?" ... Peter: "What if she's expecting someone like Tony Stark? Imagine how disappointed she'd be when she sees me." Karen: **"Well, if I were her, I wouldn't be disappointed at all."** Peter: "Thanks, Karen. It's really nice to have somebody to talk to." Peter: "Hey, how long we been here anyways?" Karen: "Thirty-seven minutes." Peter: "What?! Thirty-seven minutes? That's insane."
- AI: emotional support, gentle teaching (it sneaks the tutorial in when he is idle and curious), and naming. The user names the AI; it accepts without taking any position. It reassures him against comparison with Tony.
- Human: loneliness. Peter's mentor will not take his calls, so the AI Tony built ends up doing the mentoring. Tony did not put himself in the suit; he put in a patient, kind adult voice. That is a design statement whether or not Tony meant it.

### HC-7. The vault: the Chitauri core and the time lock (about 47%)
- V: Peter: "Hey, it's like the glowy thing." Karen: "That glowy thing is an explosive Chitauri energy core." Peter: "You mean, we've been carrying around a bomb?" Karen: "It would require radiation to transform it into an explosive state."
  Peter: "Karen, you have to help me override that time lock." ... "Okay, Karen, lower the voltage and run it." Karen: "Trial unsuccessful." Peter: "Okay, we're just gonna have to try every sequence." ... Karen: "Initiating trial 247." Peter: "It worked. It works."
- AI: identifies a hazard unprompted, then patiently brute-forces the lock overnight (the montage).
- Human: they work as partners: he sets the strategy, she does the grinding. Then: "Karen, get me to decathlon as fast as possible." Karen: "Sure thing. Just tell me where it is." ... Karen: "There's a vehicle approaching on your right." (navigation, collision warnings)

- Human: Peter's "Perfect." is comic despair. Karen's honesty is not tuned to a frightened teenager, which is the comedy, but it is also respect.

I'm trying to figure out who the guys under the bridge were that night... but I can only remember part of a license plate." Karen: "I can run facial recognition on the footage of that encounter." - Human: "Yeah, of course it is" is resigned acceptance of Tony's surveillance once he sees it is useful. Total recall was hidden from him until he needed it. That is the trade-off for a memory assistant in one scene.

- Human: blames the AI, not his own "yeah". Persuasion works once he is himself (the talk about Delmar's sandwiches).

## Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

FRIDAY appears in roughly five lines, all in the first 20 minutes. The nanotech suit (the Mark 50, in its "housing unit" on Tony's chest) is the new interface: it has no voice of its own, and you summon it by tapping it.

### IW-1. Central Park: the housing unit (about 7%)
- Tony and Pepper. P: Pepper taps the arc-reactor-shaped housing on his chest. V: Pepper: "...you wouldn't have done that." Tony: "It's just a housing unit for nanoparticles." ... "No, this is detachable." ... "I'm just trying to protect us... and future usses ... Just in case there's a monster in the closet." Pepper: "Instead of, you know... shirts."
- Meaning: the suit has gone from armour to something he wears all the time. Pepper reads it as anxiety. Same scene: "I dreamt we had a kid ... Morgan!" and "no more surprises", both of which come back in the Endgame helmet log.

### IW-2. Greenwich Village: the Q-ship arrives (about 12%)
- V: Tony: **"Friday, what am I looking at?"** FRIDAY: **"Not sure. I'm working on it."**
- V: Tony: "Friday, evac anyone south of 43rd Street. Notify first responders." FRIDAY: "Will do."
- AI: honest about what it does not know (the first time FRIDAY says she doesn't know, in contrast to the confident Zemo briefing). Then she acts as an emergency coordinator for the city, delegated by Tony with one sentence. She is effectively Tony's municipal ops centre.
- Human: Tony delegates civic action to FRIDAY with no follow-up; he trusts her completely to execute.

### IW-3. "It's nanotech" (about 14%)
- P: the suit flows over him on a tap. V: Tony to Banner: "It's nanotech. You like it?" Later in the film the suit forms weapons on demand (P: a shield, blades, a cannon, a hammer: "Hammer."). No AI voice; the suit acts as an extension of his intent (implied neural or gesture control).

### IW-4. Peter on the Q-ship: "Give me a little juice" / Iron Spider (about 17%)
- V: Peter: "Uh, Mr. Stark, I'm being beamed up." Tony: "Hang on, kid." ... Tony: **"Give me a little juice, Friday."** (P: FRIDAY reroutes power to his thrusters. The wiki calls it a "boost" that unites the propulsors.) Tony: **"Unlock 17:A."** (P: the Iron Spider suit launches from the compound and wraps itself around Peter in mid-air.) Peter: "Mr. Stark, it smells like a new car in here!" Tony: "Happy trails, kid." Tony: **"Friday, send him home."** FRIDAY: "Yep." (P: the Iron Spider deploys a parachute and Peter drops off the ship.)
- AI: power management on command; a remote delivery of hardware to a third party; and a remote override of Peter's suit to send him home, which is the Training Wheels / parental-control logic once more, now done by FRIDAY to Peter's suit.
- Human: **Peter ignores it.** P: he cuts loose and webs back onto the ship. Peter (later, V): "And this suit is ridiculously intuitive, by the way." ... Tony: "...you should not be here." Tony's remote command only lasts while the human goes along with it.

### IW-5. Pepper's call, and losing FRIDAY (about 18%)
- Initiator: FRIDAY announces the call. V: FRIDAY: **"Boss, incoming call from Miss Potts."** Pepper: "Tony? Oh, my God. Are you all right? What's going on?" Tony: "Yeah, I'm fine. I just think we might have to push our 8:30 res." ... Pepper: "Tell me you're not on that ship." ... Tony: "Honey, I'm sorry." Pepper: "Come back here, Tony. I swear to God." ... FRIDAY: **"Boss, we're losing her."** (P: the signal dies as the ship leaves the atmosphere.)
- AI: switchboard; and the last thing FRIDAY does in the film is report that she is losing the connection to Pepper. "Her" can mean the signal or Pepper. **Tony then spends the rest of the film without FRIDAY**: he is unassisted on the ship, on Titan, and during the Snap.
- Meaning: the film strips the AI away on purpose. Tony's biggest fight (Titan) has no AI voice at all; the nanotech suit is his only tech partner, and it fails (Thanos turns his own nanotech blade on him).

### IW-6. Q-ship and Titan (about 50 to 85%)
- P: Tony improvises with Strange and Peter. No AI exchanges were found. The Iron Spider's legs ("Instant Kill" mode is mentioned by fans [unverified, not in the subtitles]) are used in the Titan fight. Peter calls the suit "ridiculously intuitive."
- The end: V: Peter: "Mr. Stark? I don't feel so good." ... "I don't want to go, sir." No AI.

### IW-7. Coda
- Rhodey, at the compound with Ross: Rhodey: "You know they're only criminals because you've chosen to call them that, right, sir?" No FRIDAY scene. (FRIDAY is assumed to run the compound; the film does not show it.)

- Initiator: Tony. Modality: the Iron Man helmet as a recorder, held in his hands and recording his face. A one-way message, with no AI and nobody to answer.

- Human: humour as a coping mechanism, love said plainly. Note the echoes of IW: "no more surprises" and "it's always you."

### EG-2. The kitchen: the time-travel simulation (about 25%) [core scene]

  Tony: **"So, recommend one last sim before we pack it in for the night. This time in the shape of a Möbius strip, inverted, please."** [The MCU wiki and the subtitles say "recommend"; the clip ASR says "I'd like to run". "Recommend" probably means "re-commence".]
  FRIDAY: **"Processing..."**
  Tony: **"Give me the eigenvalue of that particle, factoring in spectral decomp."**

## Patterns

1. **FRIDAY is an operator, not a butler.** In CW, IW and Endgame every FRIDAY line is a status report, an analysis or an acknowledgement ("Sub-level five, East Wing"; "Heartbeat detected"; "Targeting system's knackered, boss"; "Not sure. I'm working on it"; "Model rendered"). She calls him "boss", where JARVIS called him "sir". There are no domestic chores, no social calendar, no talking him out of things. The one personal beat (the redhead exchange, CW-5) is Tony flirting with a face he imagines for her, and she brushes it off.
2. **FRIDAY does not question goals; she improves tactics.** In Siberia she tells Tony he can't win hand-to-hand and then gives him countermeasures (CW-7); she never says anything about the fact that he is trying to kill Bucky. JARVIS used to object to Tony's choices ("Sir, I think I need to sleep now"; the Mark II warnings, in earlier films). FRIDAY only objects to what is feasible.
3. **Unprompted action is about the body.** The cases where FRIDAY acts without being asked are all care of the body: "Multiple contusions detected", medical already called for Rhodey, "Boss, wake up", "Life functions critical". The AI's own initiative is that of a medic.
4. **Speed of delegation shows trust.** A single "Friday?" gets Ant-Man ejected (CW-3); one sentence evacuates south of 43rd Street and notifies first responders (IW-2); "Barn Door Protocol" locks down the compound. Tony uses pre-agreed names for complex actions, like protocols in a runbook.
5. **Karen is a companion built for someone less expert.** She upsells features ("Would you like me to...?"), teaches when Peter is idle (the vault), praises ("Great job, Peter. You are 98 percent successful"), reassures him about his worth ("if I were her, I wouldn't be disappointed at all"), lets him name her ("You can call me Karen if you would like"), and remembers everything (the Baby Monitor Protocol). Peter's line "It's really nice to have somebody to talk to" (HC-6) is said to the AI that stands in for the mentor who will not take his calls.
6. **Tony controls by protocol, gates and hidden telemetry.** Training Wheels (capability gated by maturity), Baby Monitor (recording by default), the tracker, the heater, "Mr. Stark is no longer connected", "Friday, send him home" (IW-4). Peter defeats each one: Ned's jailbreak, "enjoy tracking this lamp", climbing back onto the Q-ship. Karen congratulates him for "completing" a protocol he hacked. Controls without consent get routed around, and the AI does not notice the breach.
7. **The lethal option is designed to sit right next to the casual voice.** Karen offers Instant Kill on "Yeah" (HC-5) and twice more (HC-11). EDITH turns "Umm... yeah, he's a target" into "Releasing kill vehicle" and asks for confirmation only to **cancel** (FFH-4). EDITH even takes a "No!" meant for a classmate as the answer to her cancel prompt and fires. The transfer of god-level power needs only "Confirm" (FFH-6). EDITH's one objection ("You're in the strike zone") is overridden and kills its user (FFH-9). Lesson: confirmation gates are pointed at the wrong action. Irreversible, high-stakes actions need friction, and stopping should take none.
8. **EDITH is loyal to whoever holds authority, not to any values.** "Hello, Quentin" in the same warm voice; "Copy" to a kill order on children. Tony's trust was in Peter's judgment ("For the next Tony Stark, I trust you"), so the system has no judgment of its own. Peter's answer ("he didn't trust me to have EDITH, he just trusted me to pick who should") shows a delegation chain with no second check.
9. **Tony moves from builder of armour to builder of a legacy.** In CW the AI is a weapons system; in Homecoming it is a parenting proxy; in IW it is cut off (no FRIDAY in space) and the nanotech suit is part of his body; in Endgame the AI is a late-night research partner whom he reassures ("don't worry if it doesn't pan out"), the helmet becomes a love letter, and a hologram becomes his will; in FFH the AI is an inheritance carrying a note. The last word FRIDAY says in the saga ("Life functions critical") goes to Pepper, not Tony.
10. **Peter grows by taking manual control.** His arc runs from "You choose" (HC-5) through "Karen, don't ever do that again" and the near-killing of Brad to "complete manual control over detonation" (FFH-8) and "Execute them all [cancellation protocols]" (FFH-9). In both Spider-Man films, maturity is shown as needing the AI less, and using it with clear intent when he does.
11. **Pre-planned contingencies are the Stark signature.** Barn Door Protocol, Training Wheels, Baby Monitor, the EDITH bequest, the hologram "in the case of an untimely death". Tony encodes his judgment ahead of time because he will not be there. Where the encoding is careful (Karen) it protects; where it is a blank cheque (EDITH) it nearly ends in disaster.
