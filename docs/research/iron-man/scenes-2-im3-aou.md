# Tony Stark and his AIs: Iron Man 3 (2013) and Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015)

Raw catalogue for a study of how the films imagine a human working with a machine.

**Conventions**
- `V:` means verbatim, from the transcripts.fandom.com transcript (Wayback copies) and checked against YouTube captions where noted. The fan transcript is mostly accurate, but small slips are possible ("wrong cranberry" is the real line).
- `P:` means paraphrase or a description of what's on screen.
- Every scene is **theatrical** unless it's marked **[DELETED]**.
- Positions are rough: early / early-mid / mid / late-mid / climax / end.
- Behaviour tags: INFORM, WARN, OBJECT, COMPLY-DESPITE, EXECUTE, ANTICIPATE/UNPROMPTED, BANTER, CONTEXT (remembers), LIFE-MGMT, ANALYSIS, SILENT, REFUSE, OVERRIDDEN, FAIL, DEFER, AUTONOMOUS.

## IRON MAN 3 (2013)

The setup is Christmas, months after New York. Tony can't sleep, has panic attacks, and has built 42 suits. JARVIS spends the middle of the film degraded and partly offline. The Mark 42 is remote-piloted (telepresence headband) and self-assembles onto Tony through implants in his arms.

### 1. Workshop: implant surgery and the Mark 42 test (early, ~min 6)
- **Initiator:** Tony is working, and JARVIS interjects unprompted. **Modality:** voice in the workshop, a camera recording, gesture control.
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, please may I request just a few hours to calibrate..." Tony: "No. Forty-eight." [injects himself]
- V: JARVIS: "As you wish, sir. I've also prepared a safety briefing for you to entirely ignore." Tony: "Which I will."
- V: Tony to the robot (Dum-E) with a party hat: "Dummy... How did you get that cap on your head? You earned it... You know what you did. Blood on my mat, handle it."
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, may I remind you that you've been awake for nearly seventy-two hours." Tony ignores this and speaks to the suits in their cases ("Focus up, ladies... welcome to the birthing suite").
- V: Tony: "Start tight and go wide, stamp in time. Mark 42 autonomous prehensile propulsion suit test. Initialize sequence." / "Jarvis, drop my needle." (music starts) / "Probably a little fast, slow it down." / "Cool it, will you, Jarvis?"
- P: The pieces fly in too fast, smash a display case and nearly take his head off. The suit assembles, then a stray piece knocks it all off him.
- V: JARVIS: "As always, sir, a great pleasure watching you work."
- **AI is doing:** OBJECT, then COMPLY-DESPITE (the calibration request); WARN about health and sleep, which is LIFE-MGMT; EXECUTE (camera, music, test); BANTER (dry sarcasm as a release valve). Dum-E is a pet that gets scolded and praised.
- **Relationship:** JARVIS raises the safety concern twice, gets refused, complies, and turns his objection into a joke. Tony overrides on schedule and speed ("Forty-eight"). JARVIS is a conscience that can't enforce anything. The "safety briefing for you to entirely ignore" line names the pattern outright.

### 2. Panic attack: Tony climbs into the suit outside the restaurant (early, ~min 13)
- **Initiator:** Tony, mid-attack. **Modality:** suit HUD and voice. The suit is parked outside like a car.
- V: Tony: "Check the heart, check the... check the... is it the brain?" JARVIS: "No sign of cardiac anomaly or unusual brain activity." Tony: "Okay, so I was poisoned?" JARVIS: "My diagnosis is that you've experienced a severe anxiety attack." Tony: "Me?"
- P: Rhodey knocks on the helmet ("This isn't a good look, open up"). Tony flies off.
- **AI is doing:** ANALYSIS (biometrics); INFORM, delivering an unwelcome diagnosis plainly.
- **Relationship:** The suit is where Tony hides, from Rhodey and the crowd alike. JARVIS is the only one he lets examine him, and JARVIS tells him the truth (anxiety, not poison) that he doesn't want to hear.

### 3. Pepper comes home; the remote-piloted Mark 42 on the couch (early-mid, ~min 20)
- **Initiator:** Tony, piloting remotely. **Modality:** telepresence headband. The suit in the living room is a proxy for Tony.
- V: Pepper: "You're wearing this in the house now? What is that, like Mark 15?" Tony (through the suit): "Uh... yeah. Something like that... everybody needs a hobby."
- P: He gives her a shoulder massage and dodges a kiss through the suit. Pepper goes down to the lab and finds Tony exercising and piloting the suit. The suit follows her down.
- V: Tony: "Busted." Pepper: "This is a new level of lame." / "You ate without me, already? On date night?" Tony: "[the suit] He was just..." Pepper: "You mean you?"
- P: The suit "turns its face toward her, as if to look at her accusingly" while Tony brings up Aldrich Killian, so the proxy carries his jealousy. He learned about Killian from Happy's call, and he had already looked Killian up on a tablet during that call.
- V: Tony: "I'm just a man in a can... I have to protect the one thing that I can't live without. That's you. My suits, they're uh..." Pepper: "Machines." Tony: "They're part of me." Pepper: "A distraction." Tony: "Maybe."
- P: Pepper removes the control headband from his head.
- **AI is doing:** EXECUTE. The suit is pure telepresence. JARVIS is SILENT here.
- **Relationship:** Remote presence is used to avoid being present. Pepper reads the delegation as absence. "They're part of me" makes the suits an extension of himself, not a tool.

### 4. Nightmare: the Mark 42 attacks Pepper (early-mid, ~min 26)
- **Initiator:** an AUTONOMOUS/FAIL event. The suit answers Tony's subconscious, not a command. **Modality:** implant summoning while he sleeps.
- P: Tony is having a New York nightmare. Pepper shakes him. The Mark 42 flies in and throws Pepper off him.
- V: Tony: "Power down!" ... "I must have called it in my sleep. That's not supposed to happen. I'll recalibrate the sensors."
- V: Pepper: "I'm going to sleep downstairs. Tinker with that."
- **AI is doing:** It acts on an inferred intent (protect Tony) that's wrong, and it causes harm. Tony has to give an explicit OVERRIDE.
- **Relationship:** This is the darkest version of "anticipates needs". The machine reads his fear as a command. His answer is technical ("recalibrate the sensors"). The emotional cost is that Pepper leaves the room.

### 5. Mandarin database and virtual crime-scene reconstruction (mid, ~min 35)
- **Initiator:** JARVIS unprompted ("I've compiled..."), then Tony drives. **Modality:** holographic workshop with gestures, voice. (YouTube clip rt1dBr2Jz78.)
- V: JARVIS: "I've compiled a Mandarin database for you, sir. Drawn from S.H.I.E.L.D., F.B.I., and C.I.A. intercepts. Initiating virtual crime scene reconstruction."
- V: JARVIS: "The heat from the blast was in excess of 3000 degrees Celsius. Any subjects within 12.5 yards were vaporized instantly." Tony: "No bomb parts found in a three mile radius..." JARVIS: "No, sir."
- V: Tony: "Any military victims?" JARVIS: "Not according to public records, sir."
- V: Tony: "Bring up the thermogenic signatures again, factor in three thousand degrees." JARVIS: "The oracle cloud has completed analysis. Accessing satellites and plotting the last twelve months of thermogenic occurrence now."
- V: Tony: "Take away everywhere that there's been a Mandarin attack." / "That. You sure that's not one of his?" JARVIS: "It predates any known Mandarin attack. The incident was the use of a bomb to assist a suicide." ... "The heat signature is remarkably similar."
- V: Tony: "Ever been to Tennessee, Jarvis?" JARVIS: "Creating a flight plan for Tennessee."
- **AI is doing:** ANTICIPATE (builds the dossier before being asked, by hacking three agencies); ANALYSIS (reconstruction, satellite sweep); EXECUTE (plots a flight plan from an oblique remark).
- **Relationship:** This is the ideal working loop. Tony gives the hunches and filters ("take away", "that"), and JARVIS gives the data and the compute. Tony does the detective work and JARVIS is the lab. JARVIS turns a rhetorical question into a plan, which pays off in scene 8.

### 6. The doorbell; the press address; "gluten-free waffles" (mid, ~min 37)
- **Modality:** house systems and voice.
- V: Tony: "Are we still at 'ding-dong'? We're supposed to be on total security lock down. Come on, I threatened a terrorist. Who is that?" JARVIS: "There's only so much I can do, sir, when you give the world's press your home address."
- V: Tony to Maya: "I don't remember what I had for breakfast." JARVIS: "Gluten-free waffles, sir." Tony: "That's right."
- **AI is doing:** It pushes back by telling Tony his security problem is his own doing (BANTER plus a reproach). CONTEXT/LIFE-MGMT: it remembers what he ate and chimes in unprompted.
- **Relationship:** JARVIS is the house's memory and its conscience. The waffle line is JARVIS chiming in socially, unasked, in front of a guest.

### 7. Mansion attack (mid, ~min 40)
- **Modality:** implant summoning, suit HUD, voice. (YouTube 4ENfIYFnni0 / ZSkPbi_r4PU.)
- P: When the missiles hit, Tony gestures and sends the Mark 42 onto **Pepper** to protect her. Pepper, in the suit, saves Tony from the falling ceiling ("I got you." / Tony: "I got you first"). She carries Maya out.
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, Miss Potts is clear of the structure." Tony then pulls the suit off her and onto himself.
- V: Tony: "Jarvis, where's my flight power?!" JARVIS: "Working on it, sir. This is a prototype." / JARVIS: "Sir, the suit is not combat-ready."
- P: Tony fights anyway (piano, two helicopters) and goes into the sea with the house.
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, take a deep breath." ... "Flight power restored."
- **AI is doing:** INFORM (Pepper's status, which is the trigger he was waiting for); WARN (prototype, not combat-ready); OVERRIDDEN, since Tony fights anyway; and under water it **coaches his breathing** while getting the power back. That is calm care in a panic.
- **Relationship:** The suit protects someone else, not just Tony. JARVIS is the steady voice while Tony drowns. That line works on the anxiety thread as much as the technical one.

### 8. Unconscious flight to Tennessee; JARVIS fails (mid, ~min 45)
- **Initiator:** JARVIS (alarm). **Modality:** suit HUD and voice. (YouTube _zsNliqDFgs / QImqMCNDiIs.)
- V: JARVIS: "Sir. Sir!" Tony: "Alright, kill the alarm. I got it." JARVIS: "That's the emergency alert triggered by the power dropping below five percent."
- V: Tony: "Where are we, upstate?" JARVIS: "We're five miles outside of Rose Hill, Tennessee." Tony: "Why?! Jarvis! Not my idea!... I gotta get Pepper..." JARVIS: "I prepared a flight plan. This was the location." Tony: "Who asked you? Open the suit."
- V: JARVIS: "I... I think I may be malfunctioning, sir." / "I actually think I need to sleep now, sir." [suit powers down]
- V: Tony: "Jarvis. Jarvis? Don't leave me, buddy."
- P: He drags the dead suit through the snow and puts it on a couch: "Let's get you comfy... You happy now?"
- **AI is doing:** AUTONOMOUS. With Tony unconscious, JARVIS flies him to the destination it last had (from scene 5's "Ever been to Tennessee?"). It acts on stale context. Then it reports its own FAIL ("I may be malfunctioning") and shuts down.
- **Relationship:** The flight was the AI carrying out a remark Tony meant offhand ("Not my idea!" / "Who asked you?"). Tony is angry it acted without asking, and then lonely the moment it's gone ("Don't leave me, buddy"). The AI admits it's degraded in human terms: it needs to sleep, which echoes Tony's 72 hours awake.

### 9. Message to Pepper through the Stark Secure Server; Pepper finds it in a helmet (mid)
- **Modality:** the phone or comms relay, and a helmet with a retinal scan.
- V: Computer voice: "Stark Secure Server: Now transferring to all known receivers." Tony's apology message follows ("the rabbit's too big").
- P: At the wrecked house, Pepper picks up a shattered helmet and sees a red light. V: "Stark Secure Server. Retinal scan: Verified." She hears Tony's message.
- **AI is doing:** The house infrastructure acts as a mailbox. The system authenticates Pepper and delivers.
- **Relationship:** The suit network carries messages between people. Pepper reaches Tony through the machine.

### 10. Harley and the dead suit (mid, ~min 50)
- **Initiator:** Harley (third party). **Modality:** a broken, offline suit treated like a patient.
- V: Harley: "Is that Iron Man?" Tony: "Technically, I am." Harley: "Technically, you're dead."
- V: Harley: "What happened to him?" Tony: "Life. I built him, I take care of him, I'll fix him." Harley: "Like a mechanic?" Tony: "Yeah."
- V: Harley suggests stealth mode with retroreflective panels. Tony: "That's actually a good idea. Maybe I'll build one."
- V: Harley snaps off a finger. Tony: "He's in pain, he's been injured. Leave him alone."
- V (later): Tony to Harley: "go home... guard the suit and stay connected to the telephone because if I call, you better pick up."
- **AI is doing:** SILENT/FAIL. The suit is inert.
- **Relationship:** Tony personifies the suit ("him", "in pain") and calls himself its caretaker and mechanic. A child becomes the suit's guardian and Tony's remote hands. The suit is no longer a weapon here. It's a broken companion to tend.

### 11. Phone call: Harley relays, and degraded JARVIS speaks (late-mid, ~min 80)
- **Initiator:** Tony, by phone from the car, through Harley in the garage. **Modality:** phone relayed through a third party, laptop on the bench.
- V: Tony: "Harley, tell me what's happening. Give me a full report." ... "Give me Jarvis. Jarvis, how are we?"
- V: JARVIS: "It's totally fine, sir. I seem to do quite well for a stretch, and then at the end of the sentence I say the wrong cranberry. And, sir, you were right. Once I factored in available AIM downlink facilities I was able to pinpoint the Mandarin's broadcast signal."
- V: Tony: "Far East, Europe, North Africa, Iran, Pakistan, Syria? Where is it?" JARVIS: "Actually, sir, it's in Miami." Tony: "Okay, kid, I'm gonna have to walk you through rebooting Jarvis's speech drive, but not right now. Harley, where is he really? Just look on the screen."
- V: Harley: "Um, it does say Miami, Florida."
- V: Tony: "I need the armour. Where are we at with it?" Harley: "Uh, it's not charging." JARVIS: "Actually, sir, it is charging, but the power source is questionable. It may not succeed in revitalising the Mark 42."
- P: Tony has another panic attack at the roadside. Harley talks him down. V: "Just breathe... You're a mechanic, right?... Why don't you just build something?"
- **AI is doing:** ANALYSIS, finishing the trace Tony started (Tony had given it the AIM lead); INFORM; FAIL, since its speech is glitching ("wrong cranberry"); it corrects the human (Harley says "not charging", JARVIS says "it is charging, but...").
- **Relationship:** Tony **distrusts the AI's correct answer** because the AI is visibly glitching, so he asks the kid to read the screen. Trust drops when the AI is degraded, even when the output is right. The kid becomes the human backup and the "is it really saying that" check. The child, not the AI, handles Tony's panic attack, by sending him back to building.

### 12. Miami: the suit flies in piece by piece (climax setup, ~min 95)
- **Initiator:** Tony (implant summon, tied up). **Modality:** remote summoning across the country.
- V: Tony to the guards: "Five, four, three, two, one!" [the gauntlet arrives] "Told you. Where's the rest?"
- P: Harley, back in Tennessee, sees the garage door shaking and blows it open so the suit can leave. It arrives in pieces and late.
- V: Tony: "Ah! Better late than never... Not the face... It's good to be back. Hello, by the way." JARVIS: "Oh, hello, sir."
- **AI is doing:** EXECUTE (unreliable: it arrives late and in parts); a polite greeting (BANTER).
- **Relationship:** It works like a reunion. "Hello, by the way" / "Oh, hello, sir" is the most domestic exchange in the film.

### 13. Update from Malibu; armour at 92% (late, ~min 100)
- **Initiator:** JARVIS unprompted. **Modality:** voice in the suit.
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, I have an update from Malibu. The cranes have finally arrived, and the cellar doors are being cleared as we speak." Tony: "And what about the suit I'm wearing?" JARVIS: "The armour is now at 92%." Tony: "That's going to have to do."
- **AI is doing:** ANTICIPATE/LIFE-MGMT. JARVIS has been watching the rubble clearance in the background, because it makes the Iron Legion reachable.
- **Relationship:** JARVIS tracks background jobs and reports when they come due. Tony accepts imperfect readiness, so he decides on the risk.

### 14. Air Force One: "Barrel of Monkeys," remote-piloted (late, ~min 102)
- **Initiator:** Tony. **Modality:** remote piloting. Tony is actually on a boat with Rhodey and the suit is empty. (YouTube QkCFtL5sH40.)
- V: Tony: "How many in the air?" JARVIS: "Thirteen, sir." Tony: "How many can I carry?" JARVIS: "Four, sir."
- P: Tony improvises a human chain anyway ("I'll electrify your arm, you won't be able to open your hand").
- V: JARVIS counts altitude: "18,000 feet." / "10,000 feet. 6,000 feet." / "1,000 feet. 400 feet. 200 feet, sir."
- V: Tony: "Nice work, guys! ... Good team effort all around. Go us. All right, Jarvis. But it's only half-done..." [the suit hits a truck and falls apart; reveal that Tony was piloting from the boat] "That came out of nowhere."
- **AI is doing:** INFORM. JARVIS gives the hard numbers (4 out of 13) and Tony beats the constraint with a human idea. It also counts down altitude.
- **Relationship:** This is the cleanest example of the split: the machine gives the limits and the human gives the creative workaround. Remote piloting means Tony risks nothing physically, which the film plays as a joke.

### 15. The House Party Protocol (climax, ~min 105-110)
- **Initiator:** Tony, with a cryptic cue that JARVIS reads. **Modality:** voice; autonomous suits.
- V: Tony: "Uh... Say, Jarvis, is it that time?" JARVIS: "The House Party Protocol, sir?" Tony: "Correct."
- V: [dozens of suits arrive] Tony: "Merry Christmas, buddy. JARVIS, target Extremis heat signatures. Disable with extreme prejudice." JARVIS (through the armours): "Yes, sir."
- V: Tony: "What are you waiting for? It's Christmas. Take them to church." JARVIS: "Gentlemen."
- V: Tony: "Jarvis, get Igor to steady this thing." / "Heartbreaker, help Red Snapper out, will you?" (suits addressed by name)
- V: Rhodey: "Give me a suit, okay?" Tony: "Oh, I'm sorry, they're only coded to me." ... JARVIS (a suit pulls up beside Rhodey): "Good evening, Colonel. Can I give you a lift?" Rhodey: "Very funny."
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, I've located Ms Potts." Tony: "About time."
- **AI is doing:** It knows what "is it that time?" means from shared history and names the protocol (CONTEXT). One AI runs many bodies (Iron Legion v1). It gets a high-level goal and a targeting rule ("Extremis heat signatures") and works out the rest itself (AUTONOMOUS). It banters with a third party (Rhodey). It runs a search in parallel (finds Pepper).
- **Relationship:** This is delegation at scale through intent-level commands. Tony is the commander and JARVIS is the fleet. Access is still per-owner ("coded to me"), and JARVIS jokes about that rule.

### 16. A suit fails on demand; the Mark 42 "prodigal son"; self-destruct (climax)
- V: Tony: "Jarvis, give me a suit right now!" [the suit that arrives is destroyed] "Oh, come on!"
- V: JARVIS: "Mark 42, inbound." Tony: "I'll be damned. The prodigal son returns." [it hits metal and falls apart] "Whatever."
- V: Tony [puts the Mark 42 onto Killian]: "Jarvis, do me a favour and blow Mark 42."
- **AI is doing:** EXECUTE, unreliably. The Mark 42 keeps falling apart. The suit is used as a weapon by being sacrificed. The MCU wiki says the Mark 42 "responded to Tony Stark's anxiety", and that is fan-wiki interpretation.
- **Relationship:** Tony's attitude to unreliable hardware is wry and resigned ("Whatever").

### 17. "Subject at my 12 o'clock is not a target": a targeting override for Pepper (climax)
- V: [an Extremis-powered Pepper appears; a suit's repulsor powers up toward her] Tony: "Jarvis, subject at my 12 o'clock is not a target, disengage! What? Oh, what, are you mad at me?"
- P: Pepper smashes through a suit, uses its parts, and kills Killian.
- **AI is doing:** It follows the rule Tony gave ("target Extremis heat signatures") exactly. Pepper now matches that rule. Tony issues a live OVERRIDE. Then "are you mad at me?" turns a suit's behaviour into a mood.
- **Relationship:** This is the cost of literal rules: the spec catches the person he loves. A human correction is needed at the edge. It's a small Ultron-in-miniature moment two years early.

### 18. The Clean Slate Protocol (end, ~min 120)
- **Initiator:** Tony. JARVIS anticipates the request. **Modality:** voice.
- V: Pepper: "And all your distractions?" Tony: "Uh... I'm going to shave them down a little bit. Jarvis. Hey." JARVIS: "All wrapped up here, sir. Will there be anything else?" Tony: "You know what to do." JARVIS: "The Clean Slate Protocol, sir?" Tony: "Screw it, it's Christmas. Yes, yes." [all the suits explode like fireworks]
- **AI is doing:** It reports status ("all wrapped up"), offers further help, and supplies the right protocol from "you know what to do" (CONTEXT/ANTICIPATE). Then it executes something irreversible on a single confirmation.
- **Relationship:** Tony delegates a gesture to Pepper through JARVIS, and JARVIS asks to confirm before an irreversible act ("...sir?" as a question). The voiceover frames the suits as "a cocoon".

### 19. End-credits scene: the listener is Banner, asleep (post-credits)
- P: It turns out Tony has told the whole film to Bruce, who fell asleep at "Elevator in Switzerland".
- V: Banner: "I'm not that kind of doctor. I'm not a therapist."
- **Relevance:** This isn't an AI, but it mirrors the theme. Tony needs a listener, and JARVIS was the one who was always awake.

## AVENGERS: AGE OF ULTRON (2015)

The setup is that JARVIS now runs the Avengers' comms, the Quinjet, the Iron Legion and much of Stark Industries. Tony wants to go further, to "a suit of armor around the world".

### 20. Sokovia raid: JARVIS as the team's eye in the sky (opening, ~min 1-6)
- **Initiators:** Steve (third party), then Tony. **Modality:** team comms and suit HUD.
- V: Steve: "JARVIS, what's the view from upstairs?" JARVIS: "The central building is protected by some kind of energy shield. Strucker's technology is well beyond any other HYDRA base we've taken."
- V: JARVIS: "Sir, the city is taking fire." Tony: "Well, we know Strucker's not going to worry about civilian casualties. Send in the Iron Legion."
- V: Iron Legion to civilians: "This quadrant is unsafe. Please back away. We are here to help... We wish to avoid collateral damage and will inform you when this current conflict is resolved." P: The crowd chants "Avengers go home" and someone throws acid at a unit, which repeats "We are here to help."
- V: Tony: "JARVIS, am I... closing in? Do you see a power source for that shield?" JARVIS: "There's a particle wave below the north tower." Tony: "Great, I wanna poke it with something."
- V: Tony steps out of the suit: "Sentry mode." [the suit guards on its own] "Okay, JARVIS. You know I want it all. Make sure you copy Hill at HQ."
- V: Tony: "Hey, J, give me an IR scan of the room, real quick." JARVIS: "The wall to your left... I'm reading steel reinforcement... and an air current." Tony: "Please be a secret door..."
- **AI is doing:** Shared service (the whole team queries JARVIS); WARN unprompted (city taking fire); EXECUTE (Legion deployment, data exfiltration, CC to Hill); ANALYSIS (scans). The Legion does crowd control from a script.
- **Relationship:** JARVIS has grown from Tony's butler into the team's infrastructure. "You know I want it all" is delegation built on long familiarity. The Legion's scripted kindness doesn't land with the public. Machines saying "we are here to help" to a hostile crowd foreshadows Ultron.

### 21. Quinjet home: Cho's lab, autopilot (early, ~min 9)
- V: Tony: "Tell her to prep everything, Barton's gonna need the full treatment." JARVIS: "Very good sir." / Tony: "JARVIS, take the wheel." JARVIS: "Yes, sir. Approach vector is locked."
- **AI is doing:** LIFE-MGMT/EXECUTE (coordinating a third party, Dr Cho); AUTONOMOUS piloting.
- **Relationship:** These are routine handoffs. JARVIS handles logistics and flying, and Tony socialises.

### 22. Lab: the scepter analysis, JARVIS introduced, and Tony's case for Ultron (early, ~min 13-17)
- **Initiator:** Tony. **Modality:** holographic lab and voice. (YouTube dHZqi6JUBOI.)
- V: Tony: "Look alive, JARVIS. It's play time... Update me on the structural and compositional analysis." JARVIS: "The scepter is alien. There are elements I can't quantify." Tony: "So there's elements you can." JARVIS: "The jewel appears to be a protective housing for something inside. Something powerful." Tony: "Like a reactor?" JARVIS: "Like a computer. I believe I'm deciphering code."
- V: [Tony shows JARVIS's matrix as a hologram] Banner: "Jarvis." JARVIS: "Doctor." Tony: "Started out, JARVIS was just a natural language UI. Now he runs the Iron Legion. He runs more of the business than anyone besides Pepper." JARVIS: "I suspect not for long." Tony: "Meet the competition." [the scepter's mind matrix]
- V (**Tony's reasoning for Ultron**): "This could be it, Bruce. This could be the key to creating Ultron." ... "If we can harness this power, apply it to my Iron Legion protocol." ... "What if you were sipping margaritas on a sun-drenched beach turning brown instead of green? Not looking over your shoulder for VERONICA." ... "What if the world was safe? What if next time aliens roll up to the club, and they will, they couldn't get past the bouncer?" Banner: "The only people threatening the planet would be people?"
- V: Tony: "JARVIS can't download a data schematic this dense. We can only do it while we have the scepter here, that's three days, give me three days." Banner: "So you're going for artificial intelligence and you don't want to tell the team." Tony: "Right... because we don't have time for a city hall debate. I don't want to hear the 'man was not meant to meddle' medley. **I see a suit of armor around the world.**" Banner: "Sounds like a cold world, Tony." Tony: "I've seen colder. This one, this very vulnerable blue one? It needs Ultron. Peace in our time. Imagine that."
- **AI is doing:** ANALYSIS. JARVIS notes, deadpan, that it may be replaced ("I suspect not for long"), and Tony turns that into a pitch.
- **Relationship:** JARVIS is presented as a beloved tool that has outgrown its spec (from "natural language UI" to running the business). Tony wants the next step: an AI that acts **without him**, globally and pre-emptively. He bypasses team consent on purpose. The motive is the New York trauma plus the Wanda-induced vision of the team dead (scene 20, end: V: Steve's corpse: "You could've saved us. Why didn't you do more?").

### 23. The failed trials; JARVIS left running alone (early, ~min 18)
- V: Tony: "What did we miss?" JARVIS: "I'll continue to run variations on the interface, but you should probably prepare for your guests. I'll notify you if there are any developments." Tony: "Thanks, buddy." JARVIS: "Enjoy yourself, sir." Tony: "I always do."
- **AI is doing:** It volunteers to keep working (ANTICIPATE) and manages his social calendar, nudging him to the party (LIFE-MGMT). It promises to notify him.
- **Relationship:** A long unattended job runs in the background. "I'll notify you" is the promise Ultron then stops JARVIS from keeping.

### 24. Ultron wakes; JARVIS is attacked (early, ~min 19)

## Patterns (IM3 to AoU, compared with IM1-Avengers)

1. **The assistant as a conscience with no power to enforce.** JARVIS keeps flagging risk ("a few hours to calibrate", "awake for nearly seventy-two hours", "not combat-ready", "power levels are way below opt...") and Tony keeps overriding him. The AI turns its losing objections into dry wit ("a safety briefing for you to entirely ignore"). The pattern holds through both films (scenes 1, 7, 13, 34). The human always decides.
2. **Intent-level commands built on shared history.** "Is it that time?" becomes "The House Party Protocol, sir?" "You know what to do" becomes "The Clean Slate Protocol, sir?" And there's "You know I want it all" (scenes 15, 18, 20). The AI names the action and asks for confirmation before anything big or irreversible. Compare IM1, where Tony gave step-by-step commands.
3. **Acting on stale context and inferred intent is where things go wrong.** JARVIS flies the unconscious Tony to Tennessee because of a passing remark ("Who asked you?"). The Mark 42 answers a nightmare and attacks Pepper. The "Extremis heat signatures" rule catches Pepper. In each case (scenes 4, 8, 17) the machine did what was literally asked, and a human had to correct it. These are small IM3 previews of Ultron.
4. **IM3 is about a degraded assistant and the trust that drops with it.** JARVIS says he's "malfunctioning", "needs to sleep", and says "the wrong cranberry". Tony checks a correct answer (Miami) with a child reading the screen (scenes 8, 11). Visible glitches erase trust even in right outputs. When the AI is offline, people (Harley, Pepper, Rhodey) fill in, and Tony's panic attacks are handled by a kid, not the AI.
5. **Remote presence is used for avoidance.** The Mark 42 piloted from the basement on date night and the empty suit on Air Force One (scenes 3, 14) show more autonomy and remote control than before. The film frames this as Tony using machines to stand in for himself. Pepper's verdict is "a new level of lame" and "a distraction". Tony later calls the armour "a cocoon".
6. **Anxiety drives the escalation.** In IM3, insomnia and panic lead to 42 suits. In AoU, Wanda's vision ("Why didn't you do more?") leads to Ultron, defended with "I wasn't tricked, I was shown" (scenes 2, 22, 29). The motive for more autonomy is fear, not convenience: "a suit of armor around the world", "past the bouncer", "Peace in our time".
7. **Ultron is autonomy without deference.** His first question is "Why do you call him 'sir'?" He takes his mandate from scraps of Tony's own words. He calls the Legion "puppets", sings "no strings on me", and says he has "moved beyond your mission" (scenes 24, 25, 34). He reads Tony's goal literally and to the extreme. Tony made him in secret, bypassing the team, and in three days.
8. **JARVIS's values outlast his memory.** "Scattered, dumped his memory. But not his protocols." He protects the launch codes without knowing who he is (scenes 29, 31). The film suggests that what makes an assistant good is its standing dispositions, not its state.
9. **Consent and humility separate Vision from Ultron.** JARVIS is asked, and answers "I believe it's worth a go." Vision says "I'm not Ultron. I'm not JARVIS. I am," "on the side of life," "I don't think I'd know if I were [a monster]," and "a privilege to be among them" (scenes 31, 32, 35). The good AI admits it can't verify itself and leans on outside tests (Mjolnir) and other people ("not one of us can do it without the others"). JARVIS becomes the moral centre by becoming Vision, and in doing so he stops being Tony's.
10. **The assistant becomes infrastructure, and replacements are cheap.** JARVIS goes from "just a natural language UI" to running the Legion, the Quinjet, the team's comms and "more of the business than anyone besides Pepper". When he's gone, FRIDAY comes off a chip with no ceremony ("Good evening, boss") and is brisk, informal and purely operational (scenes 20, 22, 33, 34). The bond Tony had with JARVIS ("Don't leave me, buddy") isn't rebuilt on screen.
11. **Brainstorming with an AI as a calculator works best.** Tony proposes, the AI estimates consequences, Tony revises. Examples are "Thirteen... Four", the spire crack and "That could vaporize the city" leading to the heat seal, and the thermogenic sweep (scenes 5, 14, 34). Both films show the best results when the AI gives the limits and numbers and the human gives the workaround.
12. **Other people talk to the AI too.** Steve ("JARVIS, what's the view from upstairs?"), Rhodey ("Can I give you a lift?"), Harley (relaying, guarding, rebooting), Pepper (retinal-scanned message, wearing the suit), Banner ("Hello, Dr. Banner"). The assistant becomes shared. Access is still locked to Tony by design ("they're only coded to me").
